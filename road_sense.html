Road Sense

20 essential rules every road user in Hyderabad must know — covering lane discipline, signals, overtaking, emergencies, and more.

Road Sense Tips
1

Keep on your left, allowing vehicles from the opposite direction to pass.

2

Give way to traffic on your right, especially at road junctions and roundabouts.

3

While turning either left or right, give way to vehicles going straight.

4

While turning left, keep to the left side and turn close to the left side of the road you enter.

5

Slow down at junctions, intersections, and pedestrian crossings. Also slow down near school zones and temple areas. Give signals before any manoeuvre.

6

At junctions, do not block the free lefts.

7

In case of traffic jams, be patient. Do not cross the median and block the opposite lane.

8

Always use a helmet if on a two-wheeler and always check that the strap is fixed properly.

9

Stick to the given speed limit and remember that the speed limit is related to traffic conditions.

10

Keep enough distance from the vehicle ahead to avoid collision.

11

Yellow lines should not be crossed under any condition.

12

At junctions or intersections, never park the vehicle beyond the stop line.

13

At pedestrian crossings always remember that the pedestrian has the right of way.

14

At the signal, do not stop your vehicle on the Pedestrian Crossing.

15

Two-wheelers are meant only for two. A third person is not allowed, even children.

16

Do not move your vehicle on the amber light. Wait for the light to turn green.

17

Overtake only from the right side of a vehicle. Never overtake on bridges, narrow roads, junctions, school zones, and pedestrian crossings.

18

Do not drive in a zig-zag manner.

19

Do not honk unnecessarily and create noise pollution.

20

Do not spit on the roads.

Must Know

It is important to carry the necessary papers in the vehicle at all times for inspection by traffic police or otherwise. The following are the necessary documents for a non-transport vehicle:

  1. Valid Driving License
  2. Proof of valid certificate of registration
  3. Proof of valid certificate of insurance
  4. Proof of tax payment
  5. Fitness certificate
  6. Pollution Under Control (P.U.C) certificate
  7. Insurance cover note, tax payment receipt and renewal of registration certificate (if required) are valid evidence
  8. Attested Xerox copies of original documents are acceptable. However if the demanding authority wants to see originals, the owner must produce them within 15 days
  9. A Xerox copy of the driving license is not acceptable

In case the driving license is confiscated, the receipt given works in lieu of the license and must also be carried in original form.

Officers from the following authorities can demand vehicle documents for inspection:

  1. Police Department
  2. State Transport Authority

Basic Parking Rules
  • Do not park where traffic is flowing.
  • Do not park where you do not have a clear view for at least 50 metres in both directions.
  • Do not park in a place that blocks another vehicle, a sidewalk, crosswalk, or road entrance.
  • Do not park on bus bays or bus stops.
  • Do not park near public entrances to hotels, theatres, or public halls when open.
  • Do not park near any intersection or roundabout.
  • Do not park in unauthorized parking areas.
  • Park on the left side in the direction of traffic.
  • Before opening the door of a parked vehicle, look around. Close the door immediately after getting down.
Parallel Parking
  1. Slowly reverse into the space, turning steering wheel anti-clockwise, ensuring you've cleared the vehicle ahead.
  2. After clearing the vehicle ahead, turn the steering wheel clockwise, taking care not to hit the vehicle behind.
  3. Bring the vehicle in line with the curb.
  4. If not parallel, drive forward to straighten with wheels straight.
  5. Pull the handbrake, shift into gear, turn off the engine.
  6. Check traffic before opening the door.
Angular Parking
  1. Check mirrors and over your shoulder for traffic behind.
  2. Give left indicator to signal your intention to park.
  3. Steer into the parking space and straighten the wheels.
  4. Keep equal distance on both sides, apply handbrake, shift into gear.
  5. To exit: check rear view mirrors, reverse slowly, taking extra care of cycles, rickshaws and pedestrians.
What Not to Do While Parking

Do not park: on a footpath, near a traffic crossing or roundabout, on the main road, where your vehicle obstructs traffic, on the wrong side of the road, or where parking is prohibited.

Driving requires tremendous concentration. Many accidents occur because of factors that disturb your focus for just a second. Avoid these at all costs.

  • Always concentrate on driving — keep your eyes on the road and hands on the steering wheel/handle.
  • Never drink and drive.
  • Never use your mobile phone while driving.
  • Don't drive in a distracted or emotionally unstable state.
  • Avoid engaging in conversations with co-passengers where possible.
  • Use your horn sparingly — only to remind others of your presence.
  • Do not eat or drink while driving. Consume food only when the vehicle is safely stopped.
  • Do not play with kids in the vehicle.
  • Never install a TV screen on the dashboard.
  • Avoid using earphones while riding a bike.
  • Do not drive when you feel sleepy or fatigued.

Know what to do when the unexpected happens — steering failure, brake failure, or tyre burst. Be a smart driver and keep these tips at your fingertips.

Steering Failure
  • Reduce speed immediately.
  • Apply brakes steadily.
Brake Failure
  • Do not panic — panic prevents correct action.
  • Take your foot off the accelerator and rapidly change gear down.
  • Use the handbrake.
  • Steer to the side of the road.
Tyre Burst
  • Hold the steering wheel tightly to control the pulling and bring the car to a halt.
Precautions at Junctions
  • At junctions (intersections), stop, look, then enter.
  • Always give way to traffic on the main road.
  • At the intersection of two main roads, give way to traffic from your right.
  • At all roundabouts, traffic on the right has preference.
Skidding
  • While skidding, do not apply the brake — gently steer in the direction in which the vehicle is pulling.

Traffic lights indicate when it is safe to drive, ride, or walk using a universal color code.

SignalMeaning
RedSTOP — stop well before the stop line. Turn left on red only when a sign permits; yield to pedestrians and vehicles from other directions.
Amber (Yellow)GET READY — clear the road when the signal changes from green to red. If caught in the middle of a crossing, continue with care; do not accelerate in panic.
GreenGO — but check whether vehicles from other directions have cleared the road. If turning, check the signal and yield to pedestrians and vehicles.
Steady Green ArrowProceed with caution in the direction the arrow points, yielding to all pedestrians and vehicles already in the intersection.
Flashing RedCome to a complete stop. Yield to all other traffic and pedestrians, then proceed only when the way is clear.
Flashing AmberSlow down and proceed with caution.

Thousands of lives are lost on roads while overtaking. Know the rules before you overtake.

Lane Designation
  1. The lane designated for faster traffic is on the right.
  2. The lane designated for slower traffic is on the left.
  3. Overtaking is permitted to the right.
Overtaking Tips
  1. Use the right-turn indicator to signal that you want to overtake. Ensure the way ahead and behind is clear.
  2. Watch for scooters or motorcycles hidden from view and vehicles in front that may be turning right.
  3. After overtaking, indicate to get back to the same lane. Get back only when you can see the full front of the overtaken vehicle in your rear-view mirror.
  4. Never overtake on curves, road bends, or when you don't have a full view of the road ahead.
  5. Never race if the vehicle you're passing speeds up — get back to your original lane.

Note: Never overtake within 30 metres of a pedestrian crossing.

Changing Lanes
  1. Check for a space in traffic where you can enter safely.
  2. Check your blind spot by looking over your shoulder. Give a signal in the direction you want to move.
  3. Re-check to ensure the way is clear, then turn steadily into the new lane.
Overtaking at Night
  1. As you approach a vehicle from behind, switch to low beams.
  2. To warn the driver ahead, flash high beams quickly.
  3. Check mirrors and blind spot, then pull out. Switch on high beams while moving alongside.
  4. When you can see the front of the overtaken vehicle in your rear-view mirror, pull back and indicate.

Alcohol affects the Central Nervous System. Even at BAC of 0.05% the sense of judgement is impaired and the ability to control steering is affected.

A drunk driver cannot overtake safely, allow fast vehicles to pass, or coordinate with heterogeneous traffic — endangering everyone on the road.

What a Drunken Driver Lacks
  1. Alertness in perceiving and reacting to danger quickly.
  2. Accuracy of vision.
  3. A broad range of vision for events on either side of the road.
  4. Ability to perceive distance between two moving objects.
  5. Capacity to distinguish accurately between traffic light colours.
  6. Ability to drive safely during night hours.
  7. Ability to recover quickly from glare effects.
What the Motor Vehicle Act Says

Section 117 of the Motor Vehicle Act makes it an offence to drive with any quantity of alcohol in the blood or under the influence of drugs to the extent of being incapable of exercising proper control. Penalties include imprisonment up to six months and/or fine up to ₹2,000 for a first offence, and imprisonment up to three years and/or fine up to ₹3,000 for repeat offences within three years.
