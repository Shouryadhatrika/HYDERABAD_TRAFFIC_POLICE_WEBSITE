Driving License

Complete guide for Learner's Licence, Permanent DL, renewal, and more — Telangana State

Learner's Licence (LL)
Any person who wants to drive a motor vehicle on public roads must first obtain a valid Learner's Licence before applying for a permanent Driving Licence.

Permanent Driving Licence (DL)
Permanent DL is issued after successfully passing the Driving Test at the RTO. You must hold a valid Learner's Licence for at least 30 days before appearing for the test.
Driving Licence Fee Structure

Actual amounts may vary with service/user charges at the RTO.

DL Renewal₹ 200

If renewal is delayed beyond the grace period, additional penalty charges apply for each year of delay. Renew well before expiry to avoid penalties.
Renewal Process
  • 1
    Visit Sarathi Parivahan Portal or Telangana Transport Department website
  • 2
    Log in / register with your DL number and date of birth
  • 3
    Select "Renewal of DL" service and fill the application form
  • 4
    Upload required documents (address proof, photo, medical certificate if needed)
  • 5
    Pay the prescribed fee online
  • 6
    Book a slot at your nearest RTO (if physical visit is required)
  • 7
    Collect renewed DL / receive it via post

International Driving Permit (IDP)
The International Driving Permit (IDP) allows you to drive in foreign countries. It is issued by Telangana RTOs and is valid for 1 year.
Documents Required
Valid Indian DL
Passport
Valid Visa
Passport-size Photos
Application Form
Medical Certificate (sometimes)
Fee & Validity
ServiceDetail
International Driving Permit Fee₹ 1,000
Validity Period1 Year
IDP is not a standalone document — it must always be accompanied by your original Indian Driving Licence while driving abroad.
Apply Online (2026)
Telangana has fully digitized Driving Licence services. Most applications are now processed online — physical visits to the RTO are required only for biometrics and driving test slots.

How to Apply for Learner's Licence Online
  • 1
    Visit sarathi.parivahan.gov.in → Select Telangana as your state
  • 2
    Click "Apply for Learner Licence" and register / log in
  • 3
    Fill in personal details, upload Aadhaar & proof documents
  • 4
    Pay ₹200 fee online (UPI / Net Banking / Card)
  • 5
    Book a slot for the online / RTO LL Test
  • 6
    Appear for the test — pass score downloads the LL instantly
  • 7
    After 30 days, book DL driving test slot and appear at RTO
Need Assistance?

Contact the Traffic Helpline or visit your nearest traffic police station for guidance on your application.
