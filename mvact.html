Key Facts You Should Know

Drunk Driving — Zero Tolerance

Blood alcohol content above 30 mg/100 ml is an offence. Fine starts at ₹10,000 + 6 months imprisonment for first-time offenders in Telangana.

Under-18 Driving — Parents Liable

Parents or guardians face ₹25,000 fine, 3 years imprisonment, and the juvenile is barred from getting a licence until 25 years of age.

Helmet Mandatory for Both

Both rider and pillion are required to wear ISI-marked helmets. Fine is ₹1,000 with potential 3-month licence suspension for repeat violations.

Mobile Phone While Driving

Using a handheld mobile phone while driving attracts a fine of ₹1,000–₹5,000. Hands-free use is permitted but discouraged for safety.

Documents Required While Driving

Always carry: Driving Licence, RC Book, Insurance Certificate, PUC Certificate, and Permit (for commercial vehicles). DigiLocker copies are valid.

Good Samaritan Protection

Bystanders helping accident victims are legally protected from civil and criminal liability under the Good Samaritan guidelines notified by the GoI.
