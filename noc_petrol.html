Petrol Pump NOC Permission

No Objection Certificate for Petrol Pump Installation & Renewal within Hyderabad Commissionerate Limits

Official Service
Home › Services › Petrol Pump NOC
Why Permission is Required?

In order to maintain smooth flow of traffic, ease congestion, prevent danger and reduce inconvenience to the public within Hyderabad Commissionerate limits, the Commissioner of Police, Hyderabad City has issued prohibitory orders on plying of certain classes of vehicles. Persons desiring to operate a petrol pump must obtain prior NOC from the Traffic Police authority to ensure road safety compliance.

Procedure to Apply for Petrol Pump NOC
1
The applicant prepares an application addressing the Commissioner of Police, Hyderabad, along with all required documents listed below.
2
The application (in Form–IX) and supporting documents, including the site plan, LOI from the oil company, and NOC from neighbours, are submitted to the Commissioner's office.
3
The Traffic Police conduct a site inspection to verify minimum area, opening width, road width, and distance from junctions as per the prescribed norms.
4
Upon successful verification of all parameters and documents, the NOC is issued by the Commissioner of Police, Hyderabad.
