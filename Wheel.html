What Is a Wheel Clamp?

A vehicle wheel clamp (commonly known as a wheel boot) is a mechanical device fitted to one or more wheels of a stationary vehicle by traffic police to completely immobilize it.

The device locks onto the wheel hub and rim, preventing any forward or backward movement. Only authorized traffic police personnel possess the tools required to remove it — the vehicle cannot be driven until the clamp is unlocked.

Wheel clamping is governed under the Motor Vehicles Act and local GHMC/HMDA parking regulations. It is one of the most effective on-street enforcement tools available to Hyderabad Traffic Police.

Purpose of Wheel Clamping

Prevent Illegal Parking

Stops vehicles parked in restricted zones, no-parking areas, on footpaths, or in spaces causing obstruction to traffic and pedestrians.

Enforce Outstanding Fines

Ensures pending penalties and challan dues are cleared by the vehicle owner before the clamp is removed and the vehicle released.

Improve Traffic Flow

Reduces congestion and bottlenecks in busy corridors, intersections, and commercial zones caused by illegally parked vehicles.

Promote Discipline

Serves as a strong deterrent against repeat parking violations, encouraging motorists to follow designated parking norms consistently.

Protect Public Access

Keeps fire hydrant areas, emergency vehicle paths, pedestrian crossings, and disability bays clear and accessible at all times.

Enhance Road Safety

Reduces the risk of accidents caused by illegally parked vehicles that obstruct sightlines at junctions, bends, and bus stops.

How the Clamping Process Works
1

Violation Detected

A traffic police officer or enforcement squad identifies a vehicle parked illegally — in a no-parking zone, on a busy road shoulder, in a fire lane, or blocking traffic flow.

2

Documentation & Notice

The violation is photographed and logged. A challan notice is prepared citing the specific violation. A visible notice sticker is placed on the vehicle windshield informing the owner of the clamp and instructions for release.

3

Clamp Applied

The wheel clamp is securely attached to the vehicle's front or rear wheel by the authorized officer. The device is locked with a tamper-proof key mechanism, making it impossible to remove without the correct tools.

4

Owner Pays Fine

The vehicle owner contacts the traffic police helpline or visits the nearest traffic police station. The applicable fine and clamping fee must be paid — online via e-challan portal or at the station — before any removal is arranged.

5

Clamp Removed

Upon payment confirmation, an authorized officer returns to the vehicle location and removes the clamp using the official key. The owner receives a clearance receipt and may drive the vehicle away.

Legal & Safety Guidelines

Authorized Actions

  • Only licensed traffic police officers may apply or remove clamps
  • All clampings must be recorded in the official enforcement log
  • A visible notice must be placed on the vehicle window
  • Clamps must be applied carefully to avoid vehicle damage

Prohibited Actions

  • Unauthorized removal of a clamp is a criminal offence
  • Tampering with or cutting the device leads to enhanced penalties
  • Attempting to drive a clamped vehicle causes severe wheel damage
  • Private individuals or non-police personnel cannot apply clamps

Vehicle Owner Rights

  • You may request to view the violation notice and officer's ID
  • You can contest a wrong clamp through the grievance portal
  • Clamp must be removed within a reasonable time after fine payment
How to Get Your Vehicle Released
1
Note the clamp reference number on the notice sticker
Find the yellow notice placed on your windshield. It contains the challan number, officer details, violation code, and the helpline number to call. Keep a photo of this for your records.
2
Call the Traffic Helpline: 9010203626
Contact the Traffic Helpline or visit your nearest Traffic Police Station. Provide your vehicle registration number and challan number. An officer will guide you on the payment process and estimated removal time.
3
Pay the fine online or at the station
Pay via the TS Police E-Challan portal, UPI, or cash at the station. The clamping service fee (₹250) is payable in addition to the traffic fine. Keep your payment receipt or transaction ID.
4
Wait for authorized officer to arrive and remove the clamp
Once payment is confirmed, an authorized officer will be dispatched to your vehicle's location. Do not attempt to move or tamper with the clamped vehicle. Average removal time varies by location and availability of personnel.
5
Collect clearance receipt and drive away
The officer will remove the clamp and issue a clearance receipt. Inspect your vehicle for any concerns before driving. If you believe your vehicle was wrongly clamped, you may file a grievance at the station or online within 7 days.
Important Notice

Wheel clamping is a strict enforcement tool that promotes discipline and compliance with traffic laws. Never attempt to tamper with, cut, or forcibly remove a wheel clamp — this is a criminal offence under the Motor Vehicles Act and may result in arrest, vehicle impoundment, and enhanced fines. If you believe your vehicle has been wrongly clamped, please contact the Traffic Helpline at 9010203626 and raise a formal complaint.
