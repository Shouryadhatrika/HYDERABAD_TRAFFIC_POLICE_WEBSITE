Monsoon Advisory: The locations listed below are prone to water logging during heavy rainfall. Avoid these areas when possible, reduce speed significantly when passing through, and never attempt to cross flooded roads — even shallow water can be dangerous. For real-time updates, follow @HYDTP on social media or call the Traffic Helpline at 9010203626.

Monsoon Driving Safety Tips
Never cross flooded roadsEven 15 cm of fast-moving water can knock a person down. Turn around, don't drown.
Reduce speed in rainWet roads reduce tyre friction. Maintain extra distance from the vehicle ahead.
Use headlights & hazardsTurn on headlights in heavy rain for better visibility. Use hazard lights if visibility is extremely low.
Plan an alternate routeUse this list to identify waterlogged areas in advance and choose alternate roads before you travel.
Call for help if strandedIf your vehicle stalls in water, call Traffic Helpline 9010203626 or Emergency 100 immediately.
Two-wheelers: extra cautionTwo-wheelers are especially vulnerable on wet roads. Avoid waterlogged stretches entirely.
