Traffic Training Institutes

Awareness • Training • Road Safety — serving Hyderabad through structured programmes for individuals, volunteers, and transport staff.

Traffic training centres in Hyderabad, managed by the Hyderabad and Cyberabad Traffic Police, provide training, awareness programs, and education on road safety and traffic rules. Key locations include the Traffic Training Institute (TTI) in Goshamahal and Viman Nagar–Secunderabad. They conduct training for individuals, transport staff, and the Traffic Volunteers programme.
Key Traffic Training Centres
  • Traffic Training Institute, Goshamahal (TTI):
    Located at Goshamahal Road, this is a major centre for traffic training and traffic volunteer training programmes in Hyderabad.
  • TTI Begumpet (Traffic Training Institute):
    Located in Rasoolpura, this institute operates from Monday to Friday, providing regular training sessions.
Training & Services Offered
  • Traffic Volunteer Programs — Special training programs (e.g., Training of Trainers) for volunteers to educate the public.
  • Driver Safety Training — Training for commercial vehicle drivers and individuals on safe driving and traffic laws.
  • Awareness Programs — Dedicated sessions for corporates and school children.
  • Timings: 07:00 AM – 11:00 PM
Free 2-Wheeler Training Program

Free 2-wheeler training is offered at the Traffic Training Institute (TTI), Goshamahal, Hyderabad, through special initiatives for women and children in collaboration with Honda Two Wheelers India.

The facility provides structured training programmes focused on road safety awareness and riding skills.

  • Target Groups: Special programmes for women (safe riding training) and children (traffic awareness camps).
  • Collaboration: Conducted in partnership with Hyderabad Traffic Police and Honda Two Wheelers India.
  • Activities: Includes practical riding sessions and simulation tools like Honda Riding Trainers.
  • Location: Beside Police Station, Goshamahal, Hyderabad.
Note: Programmes are seasonal and require prior registration. Please contact Hyderabad Traffic Police for latest schedules.
