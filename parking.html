Basic Parking Rules
Every driver must follow these fundamental parking rules to ensure road safety and smooth traffic flow across Hyderabad.
  • Do not park any vehicle on the part of a road where traffic is flowing.
  • Do not park in a place where you do not have a clear view for at least 50 meters in both directions.
  • Do not park in a place that blocks a vehicle already parked, a sidewalk, crosswalk, pedestrian crossing, or road entrance.
  • Do not park your vehicle on bus bays or bus stops.
  • Do not park near the public entrance to a hotel, theatre, or public hall when it is open to the public.
  • Do not park near any intersection or roundabout.
  • Do not park the vehicle in unauthorized parking areas.
  • Before opening the door of the parked vehicle, look around. Close the door the moment you get down.
  • Park the vehicle on the left side in the direction of the traffic.
Parking Types & Procedures
Parallel Parking means parking the vehicle in a line (front to rear). The parking area should be one and a half times longer than the vehicle to be parked.
  • 1Slowly reverse into the space, turning the steering wheel anti-clockwise. Ensure you have cleared the vehicle ahead.
  • 2After seeing the outside rear corner of the vehicle ahead, turn the steering wheel clockwise. Look back to avoid hitting the vehicle behind.
  • 3Bring the vehicle in line with the curb.
  • 4If not parallel, drive forward to straighten. Ensure wheels are straight.
  • 5Pull the handbrake, shift into first or reverse gear. Turn off the engine and remove the key.
  • 6Check traffic before opening the door.
Angular Parking means parking at an angle to the curb. Follow these rules carefully.
  • 1Check mirrors and over the shoulder for traffic behind.
  • 2Give the left indicator to signal your intent to park.
  • 3Steer into the parking space and straighten the wheels.
  • 4Keep equal distance on both sides, pull the handbrake, shift gear.
  • 5For Exit: Check mirrors and shoulder — reverse slowly. Take extra care of cycles, rickshaws, and pedestrians.
  • Try to park in a lighted place at night.
  • If the area is completely dark, turn the parking lights on.
  • 1Be careful while passing a parked vehicle. Never open the door without looking.
  • 2Beware — a child might come in front of your vehicle suddenly.
In an emergency, if you have to stop by the side of the road, make the stop very short.
  • 1Signal your intent to pull over. Check mirrors and blind spot.
  • 2Steer to the side, stopping very close to the curb or edge.
  • 3Put on the four-way emergency lights.
  • 4Apply the handbrake.
What Not To Do While Parking
Never park your vehicle in any of the following situations — violations are punishable under the Motor Vehicles Act.
  • On a footpath
  • Near a traffic crossing, roundabout, or a turn
  • On the main road
  • Where your vehicle obstructs traffic
  • On the wrong side of the road
  • Where parking is prohibited
