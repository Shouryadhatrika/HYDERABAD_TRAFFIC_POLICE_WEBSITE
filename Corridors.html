Traffic Corridors – Hyderabad

Major expressways, ring roads, and key city corridors — mapped and explained for smarter travel across Hyderabad.

Major Traffic Corridors – Hyderabad
Outer Ring Road (ORR)
  • 8-lane expressway encircling the city (~158 km)
  • Connects airport, IT hubs, and highways
  • Fastest route for bypass traffic
Inner Ring Road
  • Connects major city zones internally
  • Reduces congestion in core areas
  • Links to ORR and radial roads
PVNR Expressway
  • Elevated expressway (~11.6 km)
  • Connects Mehdipatnam → Airport
  • Reduces travel time significantly
IT Corridor
  • Areas: HITEC City, Madhapur, Gachibowli
  • Heavy peak-hour traffic
  • Major employment zone
NH-65
  • Mumbai highway corridor
  • Key arterial route connecting western India
NH-44
  • North-South national highway
  • Longest national highway in India
Key Traffic Corridors Inside City

Punjagutta – Ameerpet – SR Nagar

High-density commercial corridor with metro congestion.

Mehdipatnam – Gachibowli

IT route with heavy office traffic.

Secunderabad – Begumpet

Central corridor with railway connectivity.

LB Nagar – Koti

Eastern dense residential traffic zone.

Uppal – Tarnaka

Student + IT mixed traffic flow.

Charminar – Nampally

Old city area with pedestrian congestion.

Traffic Tips for Citizens
⛔
Avoid Peak Hours

Travel before 9 AM or after 7 PM to avoid heavy traffic.

🚦
Follow Signals

Signal violations cause accidents and heavy fines.

🪖
Helmet / Seatbelt

Mandatory for all riders and passengers for safety.

📱
No Mobile While Driving

Avoid all distractions while behind the wheel.

🛣️
Use Service Roads

Service roads reduce main-road congestion significantly.

🚍
Use Public Transport

Metro and buses reduce individual vehicle load on roads.
