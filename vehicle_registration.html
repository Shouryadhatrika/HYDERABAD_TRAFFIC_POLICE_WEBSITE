Vehicle Registration

Complete guide for temporary & permanent registration, RC renewal, ownership transfer, NOC and more — Telangana 2026.

Temporary Registration (TR)
Temporary Registration is issued by the vehicle dealer at the time of purchase, before permanent registration is completed. Most Telangana dealers process this directly via the VAHAN portal.
Validity
1
Month
For non-transport (private) vehicles
Ext.
Extendable
Possible in special / exceptional cases
Permanent registration must be applied for and completed before the TR expires. Driving with an expired TR is an offence.
Documents Required
Form 20 Registration Application
Form 21 Sales Certificate
Form 22 Road Worthiness Certificate
Valid Insurance Certificate
Address Proof
PAN Card / Form 60
Invoice Copy
PUC Certificate (if applicable)
Temporary Registration Fees (2026)
Vehicle TypeTemporary Reg. Fee
Motor Cycle₹ 1,200
Non-Transport (Private) Vehicle₹ 1,500
Transport / Commercial Vehicle₹ 2,300

Additional smart card and service charges may apply.

Permanent Vehicle Registration (RC)
Most dealers in Telangana now process permanent registration directly online via the VAHAN portal. The RC (Registration Certificate) is issued as a smart card and dispatched by post.
Documents Required
Form 20 Application
Form 21 Sales Certificate
Form 22 Road Worthiness
Insurance Certificate
Address Proof
PAN Card / Form 60
Aadhaar Card
Invoice Copy
PUC Certificate
Customs Docs (imported vehicles)
NOC (if applicable)
Registration Fees (2026)
Vehicle TypeRegistration Fee
Motorcycle₹ 300
Imported Motorcycle₹ 2,500
LMV / Car₹ 600
Imported Car₹ 5,000
Transport / Commercial Vehicle₹ 1,000
Road Tax is levied separately and depends on vehicle cost, fuel type, category, and seating capacity. Smart card / RC issuance charges are additional.
Registration Process
  • 1
    Purchase the vehicle from a registered dealer — dealer initiates TR via VAHAN portal
  • 2
    Submit all required documents to the dealer or directly at the RTO
  • 3
    Pay registration fee, smart card charges, and road tax online
  • 4
    Vehicle inspection carried out by RTO officer (if required)
  • 5
    Registration number allotted through the VAHAN system
  • 6
    RC smart card dispatched to registered address via Speed Post

Duplicate Registration Certificate (RC)
Apply for a Duplicate RC if the original is lost, stolen, damaged, or mutilated.
Documents Required
Form 26 Application
FIR / Police Complaint
Insurance Certificate
PUC Certificate
Address Proof
Financier NOC (if hypothecation exists)
Duplicate RC Fees (2026)
Vehicle TypeFee
Motorcycle₹ 150
LMV / Car₹ 300
Process
  • 1
    File an FIR at the nearest police station if RC is lost or stolen
  • 2
    Apply via VAHAN portal or visit your nearest RTO with Form 26
  • 3
    Submit documents and pay the applicable fee
  • 4
    Duplicate RC smart card dispatched to registered address
Transfer of Ownership
Transfer of ownership is required during sale or purchase of a used vehicle. Both buyer and seller must complete the process within 30 days (14 days for buyer to report to RTO).
Forms Required
Form 29 Notice of Transfer of Ownership
Form 30 Report of Transfer of Ownership
Documents Required
Original RC
Insurance Certificate
PUC Certificate
Address Proof (both parties)
PAN Card / Form 60
NOC (if inter-state transfer)
Financier NOC (loan vehicles)
Transfer Fee
Vehicle TypeTransfer Fee
Motorcycle₹ 150
LMV / Car & Others₹ 300
Failure to transfer ownership in the prescribed time leads to legal liability remaining with the original owner in case of any traffic offence.

High Security Registration Plates (HSRP)
India has mandatory HSRP rules. All vehicles must display government-standard number plates. Fancy fonts, custom designs, and non-standard plates are illegal and subject to challan.
Number Plate Types (2026)
TS 09 AB 1234
🚗 Private Vehicles
White background · Black letters
TS 09 AB 1234
🚛 Commercial Vehicles
Yellow background · Black letters
TS 09 AB 1234
⚡ Electric Vehicles
Green plate variant
HSRP Mandatory Features
Chromium hologram
Laser-branded serial number
Snap-lock fitting (non-removable)
Standardized fonts only
Standard plate dimensions
Fancy / custom fonts — ILLEGAL
HSRP plates can be ordered through the official BookMyHSRP portal or at your nearest authorized dealer / RTO. Fitting is done by authorized vendors only.

How to Apply for RC Renewal Online
  • 1
    Visit vahan.parivahan.gov.in → Select Telangana State
  • 2
    Log in with registered mobile / Aadhaar OTP
  • 3
    Select "Renewal of Registration" and enter your vehicle registration number
  • 4
    Upload required documents (PUC, insurance, Form 25)
  • 5
    Pay renewal fee + road tax online (UPI / Net Banking / Card)
  • 6
    Book RTO slot if physical vehicle inspection is required
  • 7
    Renewed RC smart card dispatched via Speed Post
Need Assistance?

Contact the Traffic Helpline or visit your nearest traffic police station for guidance on your application.
