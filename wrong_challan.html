Why Wrong Challans May Occur?

We take every precaution to ensure that only genuine violators of the Motor Vehicles Act and traffic rules are penalized. To enhance transparency and accuracy, images of all violations — along with the date and time — are captured and securely stored in our records. This significantly reduces the chances of incorrect penalties.

However, in rare cases, errors may occur due to the following reasons:

  • Traffic personnel may unintentionally identify the wrong vehicle or individual.
  • Misuse of your vehicle registration number by another vehicle.
  • The buyer of your vehicle may not have completed the ownership transfer officially.
  • Occasional discrepancies or errors in the database records.
